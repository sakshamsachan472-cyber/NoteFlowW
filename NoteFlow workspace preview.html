NOTEFLOW HELP CENTER

Guides & tutorials

Quick, practical walkthroughs for everything in your workspace.

Start with My work →
New here? Start with these three steps
  1. Create a note or study deck
  2. Upload your class files
  3. Add a study-plan task
✎Notes, docs & courseworkWrite and organize your work
  1. Open More on Home and choose Note or Docs, or open My work and choose New doc.
  2. Add a title and your content. Documents save as you type; notes and coursework can be saved from their editor.
  3. Return to My work to search, filter, or reopen your items.
▤Study slides & presentationsCreate, edit and export a deck
  1. Choose Create study slides from Home and give your deck a name.
  2. Add slides, text, shapes, images, or tables with the presentation toolbar. Select objects on the slide to edit them.
  3. Choose Export PPTX to download a PowerPoint file. Slides are exported as images.
↥Upload & manage filesKeep class materials together
  1. Choose Upload class files on Home, or open Uploaded files and select Upload files.
  2. Pick one or more files. Each file can be up to 25 MB and is stored in your private workspace.
  3. Open Recent uploads or Uploaded files to preview, download, or manage your files.
◷Study sessions & study planMake time and track next steps
  1. On Home, choose Plan a study session to add a focused study block.
  2. In Study plan, choose Add task and enter what you want to work on.
  3. Mark tasks complete as you finish them; use the session and task panels to review your plan.
♧Teams & selected file sharingCollaborate without sharing everything
  1. Open Team to create a team or accept an invitation. Team owners can invite members.
  2. Post a request for notes, class timing, or help, or schedule a group study session so every team member can see it.
  3. Choose Choose from my uploads to copy selected personal files to the team. Your personal originals stay private.
  4. Request authors can mark their request received; owners and editors can manage shared team files.
⌕Find work & recent uploadsPick up where you left off
  1. Use the search field in My work to find notes and documents by title or content.
  2. Use the work filters to show presentations, docs, notes, or starred items.
  3. Open a file from Recent uploads, or choose View all uploads for your full file list.
